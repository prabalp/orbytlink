Refund policy

OrbytLink sells prepaid credit for AI model usage. This policy explains when you can get your money back, how to ask, and how long it takes.

The short version

  • Unused credit is refundable within 14 days of the purchase.
  • Credit that has been used is not refundable.
  • Mistakes on our side are always refunded, such as a double charge or a payment that didn't add credit.

Unused credit

Within 14 days of buying credit, you can ask for a refund of the part of that purchase you haven't used. We refund the unused share of what you paid, including the processing fee. For example, if you bought $25 of credit and used $5 of it, we refund 80% of the amount you paid, and remove $20 of credit from your balance.

When you have bought credit more than once, usage is counted against your oldest purchase first.

After 14 days, credit can't be refunded, but it stays in your account and doesn't expire while your account is open.

Payment errors

If you were charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or paid but no credit was added, we refund the payment in full, whenever you tell us. Most failed payments are reversed automatically by the bank or payment provider within 5–7 business days.

What isn't refundable

  • credit already used for model calls;
  • unused credit more than 14 days after purchase;
  • credit removed because an account was suspended for breaking our terms, such as fraud or abuse.

How to ask for a refund

Email hello@orbytlink.com from the address on your account, with the order ID shown under Settings → AI credit and the reason for the refund. We reply within 2 business days.

How you are refunded

Refunds go back to the original payment method, in the currency you paid. Once we approve a refund, it is sent within 2 business days; your bank or card issuer may then take 5–10 business days to show it. Payments in US dollars are refunded through Dodo Payments, our merchant of record for those orders, and payments in rupees through Cashfree Payments.

Cancellation

There are no subscriptions, so there is nothing to cancel and you are never charged automatically. You can stop using OrbytLink at any time.

Delivery

OrbytLink is an online service; nothing is shipped. Credit is added to your workspace as soon as the payment is confirmed, usually within a minute. If it hasn't appeared after 30 minutes, email us with your order ID.

Disputes

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank; we can usually fix it faster. If a payment is reversed through a chargeback, the matching credit is removed from your balance.

Contact

Billing questions: hello@orbytlink.com.
