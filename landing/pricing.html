Pricing

OrbytLink is free to use while it is in early access. You pay only for the AI models your agents and workflows run through OrbytLink, from prepaid credit. There are no subscriptions and nothing renews automatically.

What it costs

Gateway

Free

Connect apps, use skills and workflows, and reach everything through one /mcp endpoint. Free during early access.

AI models

Pay as you go

Models from the AI models catalog are billed per use from prepaid credit, at the provider's list price plus a 5% service fee.

Prepaid credit

Credit is bought in fixed amounts from Settings → AI credit → Add credit in the app. Credit is held in US dollars. A processing fee is added at checkout so the full amount reaches your balance.

Customers in India pay in rupees; everyone else pays in US dollars. The exact price, including any tax, is always shown before you pay, and that is the price you are charged.

How usage is charged

  • Per token. Each model call is charged for the tokens it actually uses, at the price listed for that model in the catalog.
  • Failed calls are free. If a model call fails, nothing is charged.
  • Spending limits. Workspace admins can set a monthly limit or turn AI models off.
  • Usage log. Every charge is listed in the app with its model, tokens and cost.
  • No expiry. Credit doesn't expire while your account is open.

Refunds

Unused credit can be refunded within 14 days of purchase. See the refund policy for the details.

Payments

Payments are handled by our payment partners on behalf of Orbyt Labs, which operates OrbytLink. Payments in rupees are processed by Cashfree Payments. Payments in US dollars are processed by Dodo Payments, which acts as the reseller and merchant of record for those orders and handles sales tax where it applies. We never see or store your card details.

Questions

Email hello@orbytlink.com about pricing or larger purchases.
